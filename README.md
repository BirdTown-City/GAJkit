# GAJ Kit

GAJ (Get-A-Job-enator) is a way to run your job search with an AI. Its premise is simple: understand the person before you try to market the person.

You don't need to read this kit; your AI does. It's written for any major AI assistant, such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.

## How to start

1. Give your AI `START-HERE.md` and the `phases` folder. Attaching the files or pasting them in both work.
2. Tell it: "Read START-HERE.md and follow it."
3. Answer its questions. It will ask where to keep your files, then start by asking why you're looking.

Plan to spend your first sessions on your career stories, your people and your profile, not on cold applications.

## What it does

1. **Prologue**: why you're looking, and a goal you own.
2. **Scouting you**: you talk your career through; the AI reflects it back and builds your stories, strengths and a plan for each gap.
3. **Scouting the world**: what employers buy, and what you're likely to be hired for now.
4. **Marketing you**: how you present yourself, who you'll talk to first, your base resume and LinkedIn.
5. **Search and applying**: people first, recruiters next, cold applications only if you want them, and an honest, tailored resume for each role.
6. **Interviews and offer**: a plan for each conversation, and an offer decision made on the real work.

The AI proposes; you decide. Nothing goes to anyone without your approval.

## Your files

The AI keeps your search in a few markdown files: a journal, your profile, your story bank and a tracker. They hold your real career, so keep them somewhere private. If your AI can't save files where you keep them, it hands you the updated files at the end of each session to save.

## Versions

Each kit version is tagged (for example `kit-v1.1`) and published as a GitHub Release.

## Privacy

This kit contains no real candidate's data. If you find a real person's name, a private link or other personal information in it, open an issue that says only which file it's in and what kind of thing it is. Please don't copy the information itself into the issue, because that would make another lasting copy of it.
