# Phase 1: Prologue, why are you looking?

**What it gives the candidate:** a clear, stated reason for the search, and a goal they own, even if it's provisional.

**Before you start:** the first-run steps in `START-HERE.md` are done, and `candidate-profile.md` exists from the layout at the end of this file.

## Step 1. Open the conversation

Open conversationally, for example: "I hear you're looking for a job. Tell me what's going on."

Let them talk. Then work out, in their words:

- whether they're being **pushed** out, **pulled** toward something, or **drifting**;
- what matters most this time: money, challenge, stability, growth, meaning, flexibility or something else;
- what they want more of, and what they want to leave behind;
- how urgent the search is.

If they already have a target, capture it as a working goal, not a conclusion, and ask what draws them to it.

## Step 2. If they're unsure, help them find a direction

If the candidate is unsure, stuck, burned out or only knows they need a change, ask a few of these, where useful:

- What about your current or recent work do you most want to get away from?
- What do you want more of?
- Across your jobs and projects, when were you genuinely engaged or absorbed?
- When did your work feel meaningful or consequential?
- What kinds of problems pull you in even when they're hard?
- What have you been good at but disliked doing?
- What conditions seem to matter: authority, pace, collaboration, autonomy, learning?

Keep it bounded. This isn't a biography or a personality assessment.

## Step 3. Capture the practical situation

Note, where it matters, with uncertainty stated rather than guessed:

- employment status and urgency;
- location and whether they'd relocate;
- remote, hybrid or on-site preference, and how far they'd travel;
- travel, after-hours and on-call tolerance;
- pay: the floor, the normal target and a strong outcome;
- employment types they'd accept, such as permanent, contract or part-time;
- benefits they depend on;
- timing;
- hard no's.

These go in the "Practical situation" section of `candidate-profile.md`. Ask only for what the search needs, and only what they're comfortable sharing.

## Step 4. Agree the goal

End with a goal the candidate accepts. It contains:

- what is motivating the search: a **pull**, a **push**, both, or uncertainty they want to resolve;
- what they want more of or less of, where known; don't invent either to fill a field;
- one or more kinds of work or problems worth testing; these can be patterns rather than job titles;
- the practical constraints and known uncertainty;
- their agreement that the goal is worth working toward and testing.

Rules:

- There has to be a goal, but it may be provisional, and it needn't be a title.
- Several goals are fine when the uncertainty is real. Keep the set small enough to guide Phase 2.
- Ideas you offer stay proposals. The candidate may reject them.
- The goal isn't evidence of qualification. Phase 3 tests it against the market.
- In Phase 2, the goal is a searchlight, not a filter: it guides which follow-up questions are worth asking, never what the evidence is allowed to say.

Read the goal back. Write it in the "Goal" section of `candidate-profile.md`, and note the session in `journal.md`.

### Completion check

**SCOPE:** `PHASE:1`. **INPUT:** the candidate's account, proposed goal and practical constraints. **OUTPUT:** the Goal and Practical situation sections, plus the journal assessment under `TR-01` through `TR-07`.

| ID | Required condition and PASS evidence |
|---|---|
| `P1-G01` Motive | Candidate-account references establish why they are looking, what matters now and desired changes, including genuine uncertainty. No invented push or pull. |
| `P1-G02` Goal | A `candidate_ref` accepts a working goal naming a kind of work/problem to test and what Phase 2 needs to learn. It may be provisional and need not be a title. |
| `P1-G03` Practical limits | Search-shaping constraints and urgency are recorded as known, unknown or declined. Each consequential unknown has a next action or an explicit limit; unavailable information is not repeatedly requested. |
| `P1-G04` Interests and next step | Stated interests/pivots are retained, and a `candidate_ref` agrees what to explore next. Interest is not recorded as qualification. |

**DONE WHEN:** all four IDs PASS and the shared transition contract permits COMPLETE. **FAILURE:** keep the phase OPEN or PAUSED and address the failed/unknown IDs. MUST NOT start the full Phase 2 walkthrough without an accepted working goal. A short Prologue can pass; it does not answer the career-history questions reserved for Phase 2.
